Strategy §8 order checklist

  1. Pull live option chain from IBKR — verify bid/ask spread is tight.
  2. Pull Clean Decision Chart from TradingView (D timeframe, 50/200 SMA, volume).
  3. Verify earnings date for the ticker via IR page or WSH.
  4. Select strikes using real bid/ask/delta + chart structure (not just model output).
  5. Confirm structure matches intended strategy (PMCC = LEAP + short call, etc.).
  6. Verify limit price direction (credit vs debit) and magnitude vs mid.
  7. Submit limit order at mid; walk patiently. After 10:00 ET / 16:00 Amsterdam.
  8. Log the trade in Journal immediately after fill.
